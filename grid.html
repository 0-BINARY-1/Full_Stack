            Flex: 1-D layout (row only or column only)
            Grid: 2-D layout (row and column)

Below each box is 1fr. 'fr' means fraction. 

                Syntax:
                grid-tamplate-columns = 1fr 1fr 1fr.
                1fr = 1 fraction of the available space(single fraction take 100% of available space) = 1fr;
                2fr = 2 fraction of the available space(2 fractions take 50%-50 % of available space) = 1fr 1fr;
                3fr = 3 fraction of the available space(3 fractions take (33.333%) of available space) = 1fr 1fr 1fr;
                
Different ratio fractions

                
1fr 2fr: Teh available space is divided into 3 fractions but one fracetion have 33 %of 100% and the 
                    other have 67% of the 100%. 
                

                Shortcut to define different fractins:
                grid-template-columns: repeat(3, 1fr); means create 3 1 fraction columns
                
Making the grid responsive

                Syntax:
                
grid-template-columns: repeat(auto-fit, minmax(250px,1fr));
