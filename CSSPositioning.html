There are five important position values. They are:

  1. static
  2. relative
  3. absolute
  4. fixed
  5. sticky

By default the positon is static.

Absolute Positioning

This is the parent section content.

This is where relative and absolute are commonly used together. The element is positioned relative to its nearest positioned ancestor by removing the element from the normal document flow.

Fixed Position

The content remain in that position even when scrolled like it is glued to that position of the display screen. The contact button in fixed position.

Sticky position

Sticky behaves like a normal element until you scroll to a certain position. Then it sticks to the specified position.Like at the top the navigatio bar is sticking to its place even we scroll.
